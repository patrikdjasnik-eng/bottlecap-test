# 🐇 RHAR-CEP 002 — Verified experience without unnecessary complexity

**Draft version:** v0.3, 2026-10-10. **Status: a research specification for future implementation approval; no CEP 002 code, training or GPU experiment has been performed.**  
[🇨🇿 Česky](../CZ%20docs/CEP-002.md) · [IDEA](../IDEA.md) · [DEV-NOTES](DEV-NOTES.en.md)

## 1. One objective

Teach Qwen3-0.6B to produce **fewer total generated tokens (reasoning + final)** while preserving correctness. Thinking remains enabled. The improvement must reside in the trained model / LoRA. **No external XP memory, second judge model, custom inference controller or extra runtime generations** are allowed as dependencies of official evaluation.

**Hypothesis:** before training, separate invalid attempts from verified ones, protect indispensable problem → derivation → answer dependencies, and use later verified experience to revisit and repair earlier mistakes. Train the model on **complete, short, verified procedures**, not merely shorter-looking text.

## 2. Only three components

| Component | Simple responsibility | What it does not claim |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **VPS: Verified Procedural Steps** | Verify that a procedure uses the problem conditions, performs checkable operations correctly and finishes with a valid answer. | Does not prove arbitrary free-form reasoning. |
| **XP: Procedural Experience** | Retain up to **three genuinely available independently verified** applicable strategies, choosing an efficient training target. | Does not invent a missing strategy or treat a numeric answer as a universal rule. |
| **REC: Retroactive Experience Correction** | Allow newly verified experience to flag earlier **training** traces for a proven, versioned correction. | Qwen does not automatically persist history or modify weights during normal inference. |

**Pipeline:** permitted training question → available candidates → VPS check → XP selection → optional REC revisit of older *training* records → short verified training trace → separately approved LoRA SFT → untouched holdout evaluation.

## 3. Defining a valid procedure

The **VPS MVP** covers only a few structured mathematics templates: grouped quantities, sequential percentages and small equation systems. Besides the reference answer, each training question needs a **task contract** encoding the input facts, meaning of operations, required dependencies and testable constraints. A contract may be derived from a reference rationale, but should be independently checked and manually reviewed if needed. Unsupported or ambiguous items must be excluded.

Example: “five boxes of twelve apples each; sell seventeen” requires 5 × 12 − 17 = 43. While 5 + 12 = 17 is arithmetically true, **it is not the relationship specified in the question**. A lucky correct final answer does not validate the intermediate reasoning.

Four verifier states: **VERIFIED** = requisite checkable dependencies and final answer match the problem and the generation terminates correctly; **INVALID** = a demonstrated contradiction; **UNVERIFIED** = ambiguity or unsupported reasoning; **TRUNCATED** = an unfinished generation. Only **VERIFIED** complete examples enter positive SFT. Never silently promote UNVERIFIED to VERIFIED. Avoid unsafe execution of generated expressions.

**Key design contribution — protect necessary steps:** teach the short complete template **problem facts → indispensable dependencies → check applicable constraints → Final answer → stop**. Do **not** teach “stop as soon as any plausible number appears”; do not add custom inference-time stopping logic to the first version.

## 4. XP and REC with a small compute budget

**XP:** Store at most three actually existing independently verified paths for a question/structure. One valid path is sufficient. Rank by validity and applicability first, preservation of required dependencies second, and **actual total generated tokens** only afterward. Do not blindly splice incompatible snippets into a fictional reasoning trace.

**REC:** If a newly verified procedure on a later *training* problem exposes a **specific potential conflict** in a stored earlier example, enqueue the older example as RECHECK_PENDING. Mere textual similarity is never a sufficient trigger: the proposed general rule must have compatible meanings, input types and applicability conditions. Revisit candidates once at the end of a small batch, with bounded work.

A verifier then recomputes the **original** problem's numbers and conditions. If an error and its repair are independently established, preserve the immutable original trace and append a new version with the exact mistake, evidence and applicability conditions. Track XP+ (verified correct), XP− (verified failure under stated conditions), and REC (revision history). Unresolved cases remain UNVERIFIED; never punish an example merely because it looks similar.

Example: question 3 incorrectly interprets 3/8 as one eighth. A validated procedure for question 10 correctly multiplies by three and proposes a review of question 3. The earlier solution can be corrected **only after verification against question 3's own facts**.

**MVP simplicity:** JSONL records, one purpose-built CPU verifier and one batch correction queue. No vector database, multi-agent orchestration, preference RL/DPO, permanent runtime memory or automatic online fine-tuning. In v0.2, XP− records are for diagnosis only; do not train the model to imitate erroneous traces.

### 🛡️ Verified Lock — preventing the model from learning its own mistakes (inside VPS + REC)

**Core invariant:** Qwen may propose a suspicion, but **Qwen alone may neither certify its own answer nor invalidate a previously verified one**. Verified Lock is **not a fourth subsystem**; it is a safety rule enforced in VPS and REC.

1. **Lock only after independent checks:** require trusted question/reference provenance, a separately checked task contract, all required checkable intermediate relations, a correct final answer and normal termination. Record **VERIFIED_LOCKED** with question identity, contract hash, verifier version and evidence. A matching gold number alone is insufficient; missing or ambiguous checks remain **UNVERIFIED**.
2. **Later disagreement:** question 10 may only trigger **RECHECK_PENDING** for question 3. Do **not** demote the old answer, create negative XP or promote a new correction as training data yet. Re-evaluate the **original question 3** facts independently.
3. **Original verified solution still checks out:** mark the proposal **REJECTED_CORRECTION**, preserve VERIFIED_LOCKED, record the evidence and count a false correction.
4. **Previous incorrect or unverified solution:** only when the old error is demonstrated and the **entire repaired procedure** independently passes verification, append a new versioned **VERIFIED_LOCKED** repair. Never overwrite the original trace. Label XP− only for proven failures under stated conditions.
5. **Contradictory purported proofs / changed reference or verifier:** mark **LOCK_DISPUTED / REVIEW_REQUIRED**. Never pick a winner automatically or generate new training examples from a disputed record; audit the data, contract and verifier, and require a *manually approved* versioned resolution. A lock protects against ungrounded Qwen claims; **it does not make a verifier infallible**.

**False-correction example:** five boxes × twelve apples − seventeen sold equals 43. A later generation proposes 42. Rechecking the trusted original contract yields 43, so the false proposal is rejected and the correct experience is not overwritten.

**Counters and tests:** record **false_correction_proposals**, **accepted_verified_repairs**, **rejected_corrections**, **disputed_locks**, **unverified_cases**. Unit tests must cover false corrections against previously locked correct solutions, unsupported triggers, lucky correct final answers with incorrect derivations, ambiguous contracts, verifier version changes and immutable history. Passing CPU tests does not prove that all natural-language task contracts are accurate.

## 5. Illustrative algorithm pseudocode

~~~python
for task in permitted_training_tasks:
    for solution in existing_candidates(task)[:3]:
        verdict = VPS.verify_against_trusted_contract(task, solution)
        history.append_immutable(task, solution, verdict)
        if verdict.status == "VERIFIED":
            XP.lock_verified(task, solution, verdict.evidence)
            REC.flag_possible_older_conflicts(task, solution)  # suspicion only

for old_task in REC.pending_batch():
    audit = VPS.recheck_original_task(old_task)
    if audit.old_solution_still_verified:
        REC.reject_correction(old_task, audit.evidence)  # original lock stays
    elif audit.lock_or_contract_disputed:
        REC.require_review(old_task, audit.evidence)    # no training data
    elif audit.old_error_proven and audit.repair_verified:
        REC.append_verified_repair(old_task, audit)     # never overwrite
    else:
        REC.keep_unverified(old_task)

training_targets = XP.shortest_complete_locked_traces()
~~~

## 6. One modest experiment with meaningful controls

Each arm should use the **same permitted training question IDs, base revision, and matched seeds, steps, epochs and number of training examples** wherever feasible. If CEP 002 changes the data population, retrain the ordinary SFT control on that *same* population rather than misattributing a dataset shift to REC.

- **A — ordinary SFT:** conventional correct reference rationales.
- **B — VPS + XP without REC:** verified complete concise procedural targets.
- **C — VPS + XP + REC:** identical process plus verified retroactive repairs.

To establish REC's contribution, compare **C vs B**, not just against unmodified Qwen. Existing CEP 001 and SFT 003 are useful historical references, not automatically matched experimental arms. Judge on fresh, unseen questions using **strict accuracy, all generated tokens (thinking + final), completion, false-correction rate and verifier cost**. A 10% token reduction without detectable accuracy deterioration may be an exploratory aspiration, never a promised result. Report uncertainty and limits for small holdouts.

**Green-tests gate:** start with 10–20 carefully reviewed *training* examples for CPU unit tests: correct facts/relations, incorrect relations, lucky final answer supported by wrong derivation, wrong final, truncation, ambiguity, spurious REC trigger, non-destructive versioning, **false correction rejected against a correctly locked solution**, **LOCK_DISPUTED for conflicting verifier evidence**, and preserved original history. Fail closed. Request separate approval for larger permitted data preparation, then for GPU/LoRA training; do not use an unverified procedure as a positive example.

## 7. Non-negotiable constraints

- **No contamination:** SVAMP, MBPP+, BFCL, IFEval are official evaluation only. The 60 questions already inspected in RHAR Validation 006 must not simultaneously serve as new training examples and be claimed as an independent holdout. Prefer not to reuse them for CEP 002 training.
- **BottleCapAI compatibility:** official comparison of original vs adapted Qwen on SVAMP, MBPP+, BFCL and IFEval; identical official prompts, sampling and token caps; thinking enabled. Standard Transformers/vLLM loading without a custom CEP runtime.
- **No overclaiming:** on a 60-question exploratory GSM8K split, CEP 001 achieved 23/60 correct vs SFT 19/60 and 5,266 vs 5,769 total tokens, while an earlier 20-question split did not show a similar advantage. None of this proves generalization or REC's efficacy.
- **Prior art:** selecting short correct traces, learning from mistakes and procedural memory have published precedents. CEP 002 is a research hypothesis, not established scientific novelty.

## 8. Current status and approvals

**Approved here:** document and discuss the simple VPS + XP + REC research method.  
**Out of MVP scope:** runtime memory, multi-agent systems, five parallel training strategies, RL/DPO or special CUDA kernel engineering.  
**Awaiting separate consent:** CPU verifier implementation and test suite, data preparation, new model inference, LoRA training and official benchmarks.

**In one sentence:** teach Qwen to retain necessary reasoning dependencies, learn concise verified procedures and learn from documented earlier mistakes *during offline training*, then solve new problems on its own with fewer tokens and comparable accuracy. 🐇🛡️
